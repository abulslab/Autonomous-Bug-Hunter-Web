# Demo Supply Co.: product overview

Demo Supply Co. is an online shop for outdoor, office and fitness gear.

## Who uses it
- **Visitors** browse and search the catalog.
- **Shoppers** sign in, fill a cart, apply coupons, check out, see their orders and cancel an order before it ships.
- **Staff** sign in to the order desk, see every order, and mark orders as shipped and then delivered.

## Catalog
Twelve products in six categories (Footwear, Outdoor, Electronics, Apparel, Fitness, Home). Each product shows its
price and how many are in stock; some items are low in stock and one is sold out.

## Out of scope
Card numbers are validated but no real payment is taken. The test card 4000 0000 0000 0002 is always declined.
