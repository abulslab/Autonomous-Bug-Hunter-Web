# Pricing and promotions

## Totals
- A line's total is the product's price times the quantity; the subtotal is the sum of the lines.
- Shipping is $5.99, free when the subtotal is $50 or more or with the FREESHIP coupon.
- Sales tax is 8% of (subtotal - discount), rounded to the nearest cent.
- The order total is subtotal - discount + shipping + tax.
- The price in the cart and on the order is the price shown in the product list.

## Coupons
| Code | Effect |
|---|---|
| SAVE10 | 10% off the subtotal |
| WELCOME5 | $5 off |
| FREESHIP | Free shipping |

- Each coupon applies once per cart; applying it again changes nothing.
- A discount never exceeds the subtotal. Unknown codes are rejected with a message.
