# Booking rules

## Dates
- A stay starts today or later and ends after it starts; the shortest stay is one night.
- Dates are entered as yyyy-mm-dd.

## Guests
- A booking is for at least one guest and no more than the room sleeps.

## Price
- The price of a stay is the number of nights times the room's nightly rate, the same rate the room list shows.
- Every night of a stay is charged, including the last.

## Booking status
- A new booking is **Confirmed**. The front desk checks the guest in (**CheckedIn**) and later out (**CheckedOut**).
- A guest can cancel a booking only while it is Confirmed; it then shows as **Cancelled**.
- Only front-desk staff can see other guests' bookings or change a booking's status.
