# Demo Hotel: product overview

Demo Hotel sells stays in four rooms online and runs a small front desk.

## Who uses it
- **Guests** search rooms, book a stay, see their bookings and cancel them before arrival. Guests do not sign in: a
  booking belongs to the browser it was made in.
- **Front-desk staff** sign in, see every booking, check guests in on arrival and out on departure.

## Rooms
| Room | Sleeps | Nightly rate |

## Out of scope
Payments are taken at the front desk on arrival; the website takes no card details.
