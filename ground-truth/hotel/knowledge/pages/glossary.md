# Glossary

- **Stay**: the nights between check-in and check-out dates.
- **Night**: one date of a stay; a stay from the 10th to the 13th is three nights.
- **Rate**: the price of one night in a room.
- **Front desk**: hotel staff who manage arrivals and departures.
- **Test data**: the site starts with one booking by another guest (Ada Lovelace, Family Suite), so the front desk
  always has something to show.
